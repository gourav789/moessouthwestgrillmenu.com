Use your points whenever you want. Rewards don't expire. Accumulate points across multiple transactions and combine them with other valid coupons (where applicable).

Present your printed coupon or show the coupon on your mobile phone at the register when ordering. The cashier will apply the discount to your bill.

❓ Coupons FAQs

Can I stack coupons?

Generally, most coupons are limited to one per transaction. However, Moe Rewards points may sometimes combine with other offers. Check specific coupon terms or ask in-store staff.

Do Moe Rewards points expire?

No! Moe Rewards points never expire. Accumulate them as long as you like and redeem whenever you're ready. There's no rush or time limit.

What if my coupon doesn't work?

If a coupon fails at checkout or in-store, check that it's valid at your location, hasn't expired, and meets all coupon conditions. Contact Moe's customer service or the location directly for help.

Are online coupons different from in-store?

Many coupons work both online and in-store, but some are exclusive to one channel. Always read the fine print. App-exclusive deals typically only work through the Moe's app or moes.com.

Can I use coupons with delivery apps?

Moe's coupons typically work through direct Moe's orders (app, website, in-store, phone). Third-party delivery app orders use the app's own promotions instead of Moe's coupons.

How often are new coupons released?

New coupons are released regularly — typically weekly with Moe Monday specials, plus seasonal and holiday promotions. Check the Moe's app for real-time deal updates.
