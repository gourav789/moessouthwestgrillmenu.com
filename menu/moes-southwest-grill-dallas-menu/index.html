💚 Healthy Moe's Options (Dallas)

Moe's is an excellent choice for healthy eating. All items are prepared fresh and customizable. Here are strategies to reduce calories, sodium, and fat while maximizing protein and nutrition.

🥣 Choose Bowls Over Burritos

Bowls are typically 100-150 calories less than burritos because they skip the tortilla. You get more vegetables and control portions better. Add lettuce and tomato for volume without calories.

🍗 Select Grilled Chicken

Grilled chicken is the leanest protein. It's typically 100-150 calories less than carnitas and steak. High in protein, lower in fat. Best choice for calorie-conscious diners.

🥑 Skip or Reduce Guacamole

Guacamole adds 150-200 calories. Request half portion or skip entirely. Substitute with fresh lettuce, tomato, or pico de gallo for freshness without calories.

🧀 Light on Cheese & Sour Cream

Request light cheese or skip sour cream to save 100-150 calories. Add extra cilantro, lime, and salsa for flavor without the fat.

🥗 Salad with Vinaigrette

Request salad without the tortilla bowl to save 150+ calories. Ask for vinaigrette-based dressing instead of creamy ranch. Load up on vegetables.

🌶️ Load Up on Salsa & Cilantro

Fresh salsa and cilantro are virtually calorie-free and packed with flavor. Add extra jalapeños, lime, and pico de gallo for authentic taste without guilt.

💪 Pro Tips: Ask for extra vegetables (lettuce, tomato, peppers) to increase volume and satisfaction. Choose cilantro-lime rice for extra flavor without added calories. Drink water instead of sugary sodas. Request dressing on the side for portion control. Most Moe's staff are happy to customize—don't hesitate to ask for modifications.
