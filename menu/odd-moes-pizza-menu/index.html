📖 1. The Story of Odd Moe's Pizza

In a world dominated by massive, corporate pizza chains, Odd Moe's Pizza has carved out a unique and highly loyal following in Oregon's Willamette Valley. Founded on May 7, 1999, by Michael Sublett and Samuel Wibowo, the company started as a humble local operation with a commitment to high-quality ingredients, enormous portions, and unparalleled customer service.

Interestingly, the chain didn't originally start with the name "Odd Moe's." The moniker was adopted later, inspired by a quirky mascot that represented the brand's fun, slightly eccentric, and highly community-focused ethos. Over the past 27 years, Odd Moe's has expanded across multiple cities including Salem, Keizer, Canby, McMinnville, and Newberg.

The Odd Moe's Difference: What truly separates Odd Moe's from the competition isn't just their heavily loaded gourmet pizzas or their rich, baked pastas—it is their pioneering approach to delivery. Long before third-party apps made it commonplace, Odd Moe's became famous in Oregon for delivering cold beer and wine right alongside your piping hot pizza.

🍗 4. Wings & Appetizers

No pizza night is complete without sides, and Odd Moe's treats their appetizers with the same respect as their main courses.

  • Bone-In Wings: Oven-baked to a perfect crisp. Available in Spicy Buffalo, BBQ, or Sweet Chili. (Usually priced around $12.00 - $14.00 for a dozen).
  • Boneless Wings: Tender chunks of white meat chicken breaded and baked, tossed in your choice of sauce.
  • Cheesy Garlic Bread: Thick-cut bread slathered in garlic butter and smothered in mozzarella. Served with marinara dipping sauce.
  • Potato Wedges: Thick, seasoned wedges baked until golden brown. A great alternative to traditional french fries.
  • BBQ Ribs: A unique offering for a pizza joint! Slow-cooked, tender baby back ribs slathered in sweet and tangy BBQ sauce.

🍻 6. Beer & Wine Delivery Services

One of the most famous aspects of Odd Moe's Pizza is their alcohol delivery service. They were pioneers in this space in Oregon, allowing customers to get a six-pack of cold craft beer delivered right to their front porch alongside their pepperoni pizza.

  • The Selection: They offer a rotating variety of local Oregon craft beers (IPAs, Ambers, Stouts), domestic favorites (Coors Light, Budweiser), hard ciders, and select wines.
  • How it Works: You can add alcohol to your order online or over the phone.
  • The Law: You MUST be 21 or older to order. The delivery driver will require a valid, government-issued photo ID upon delivery. If you cannot produce an ID, or if you appear intoxicated, they are legally required to refuse the alcohol delivery.

❓ 8. Extensive Frequently Asked Questions

Got more questions about ordering from Odd Moe's? We have compiled the most frequently asked questions regarding their menu, dietary options, and delivery policies.

1. Does Odd Moe's offer Gluten-Free Pizza?

Yes! Odd Moe's offers a Gluten-Free crust option. However, because they operate in a kitchen heavily saturated with airborne flour from their standard dough, it is NOT recommended for individuals with severe Celiac disease due to the high risk of cross-contamination. It is great for those with mild gluten sensitivities or dietary preferences.

2. Can I order Odd Moe's on DoorDash or UberEats?

Yes, most Odd Moe's locations partner with major third-party delivery apps. However, ordering directly through their official website often saves you money on markup fees and ensures better customer service.

3. Do they serve vegan options?

While they do not currently advertise a dedicated vegan cheese, you can easily order a "Borg" (Veggie) pizza without cheese and load it up with their fresh vegetable toppings over traditional red sauce.

4. What is the difference between The Lineman and The Grand Salami?

The Lineman is a traditional "Supreme" or "Combo" pizza, featuring both meats (pepperoni, sausage, salami) and veggies (mushrooms, olives, onions, peppers). The Grand Salami is strictly an "All-Meat" pizza (no veggies) loaded with five different types of meat.

5. Do I have to buy food to get beer delivered?

Yes. Due to Oregon state liquor laws and delivery regulations, alcohol purchases must generally accompany a food order of a certain minimum value.

6. Are the wings fried or baked?

Odd Moe's wings are oven-baked to ensure a healthier profile while still maintaining a delicious, crispy exterior before being tossed in sauce.

7. Does Odd Moe's cater for large events?

Absolutely. They are a popular choice for school events, office parties, and sports team dinners. If you are ordering more than 10 large pizzas, it is highly recommended to call the store 24 hours in advance.

8. Is Odd Moe's Pizza related to Moe's Southwest Grill?

No, they are completely separate entities. Odd Moe's is a regional pizza chain in Oregon, while Moe's Southwest Grill is a national Tex-Mex chain. You can check out the Moe's Southwest Grill menu here.
