✏️ The Art of Customizing Your Burrito

One of the greatest joys of dining at Moe's is the ability to customize your burrito exactly as you envision it. Unlike some restaurants where customization feels like a hassle, Moe's embraces it as part of the core dining experience.

Step-by-Step Customization Guide

Step 1: Choose Your Base

Options:
• Burrito - 12" flour tortilla (full-size)
• Jr. Burrito - 10" flour tortilla (smaller)
• Bowl - No tortilla, same fillings

Step 2: Pick Your Protein

Choose 1 (or 2 for double):
• Adobo Chicken (lean)
• White Meat Chicken (extra lean)
• Steak (premium)
• Ground Beef (hearty)

Step 3: Select Your Rice

Choose 1:
• Cilantro Lime Rice (fresh, bright)
• Seasoned Rice (spiced)
• No Rice (low-carb option)

Step 4: Choose Your Beans

Choose 1 or both:
• Black Beans (more popular)
• Pinto Beans (classic)
• Both (maximum filling)

Step 5: Add Toppings (FREE!)

Add as many as you want:
• Pico de Gallo
• Lettuce, Cilantro
• Jalapeños, Onions
• Cheese, Sour Cream

Step 6: Choose Your Sauce

Select 1 or none:
• Poblano Crema
• Chili Lime Sauce
• Moe's Sauce
• Chipotle Ranch

Pro Tips for Building Your Perfect Burrito

  1. Don't Overload: While you can add everything, burritos that are too packed are harder to eat and can lead to spillage. Layer strategically.
  2. Layer Strategically: Ask the staff to layer heavier items (beans, rice, meat) first, then add lighter toppings and sauces on top.
  3. Go Easy on Liquids: Sour cream and sauces can make burritos soggy. Consider requesting them on the side.
  4. Double Protein = Double Flavor: The Homewrecker proves that two proteins can be better than one. Combine complementary flavors.
  5. Fresh Ingredients Matter: Opt for fresh toppings like pico de gallo and cilantro over heavier options.
  6. Sauce Placement: Request sauce on the side or drizzled strategically to prevent sogginess while maintaining flavor.
  7. Don't Skip the Free Chips: The complimentary chips and salsa aren't an afterthought—they're part of the premium experience.
  8. Request Customizations: Staff is trained to handle special requests. Ask for modifications without hesitation. If you have dietary restrictions, check out our Moe's Allergen Menu Guide.
