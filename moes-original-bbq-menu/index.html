📖 Overview: What is Moe's Original BBQ?

If you are searching for authentic, down-home Southern barbecue, Moe's Original BBQ is a standout destination. Specializing in Alabama-style barbecue, Moe's is famous for its fruit-wood smoked meats, tangy Alabama white sauce, and incredible scratch-made Southern sides.

With over 50 locations across more than a dozen states, this fast-casual joint delivers a relaxed, blues-infused atmosphere perfect for lunch, dinner, or grabbing a "Haul it Home" family pack. It is essential to note that Moe's Original BBQ is entirely distinct from Moe's Southwest Grill. If you are craving fresh Tex-Mex burritos instead of ribs and pulled pork, head over to our Moe's Southwest Grill Menu homepage.

🕰️ A Brief History of Moe's Original BBQ

The story of Moe's Original BBQ begins in 1988 at the University of Alabama in Tuscaloosa. Three friends—Mike Fernandez, Ben Gilbert, and Jeff Kennedy—bonded over a mutual love for Southern BBQ, cold beer, and blues music.

After learning how to fire-roast meats from Moses Day, a legendary BBQ pitmaster in Tuscaloosa (the namesake "Moe"), the trio eventually relocated to Vail, Colorado. What started as a roadside pit quickly blossomed into a highly successful restaurant. Today, the chain brings their award-winning recipes—featuring meats smoked fresh daily and side dishes prepared from scratch—to communities nationwide while maintaining the authentic, laid-back vibe of a backyard Southern BBQ.

🎉 Catering Services

Moe's Original BBQ is highly regarded for its robust catering services, perfect for weddings, corporate lunches, tailgates, and family reunions.

  • Delivery & Setup: They will deliver the food hot and set up a buffet line with disposable chafing dishes.
  • Full Service: For weddings and upscale events, Moe's provides staff to manage the buffet line, replenish food, and clean up the serving area.
  • Unique Experiences: Some locations offer whole pig roasts or traditional Southern low-country seafood boils cooked on-site!

💡 Booking Tip: Catering prices usually run between $12 to $18 per person depending on the meat selections. It is best to contact your local Moe's Original BBQ at least a week in advance for large orders.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What does "Bama Style" mean?

At Moe's Original BBQ, ordering a sandwich "Bama Style" means it comes topped with their signature red BBQ sauce, marinated slaw, and dill pickles directly on the meat inside the bun. It provides the perfect crunch and tang to cut through the rich smoked meat.

What is Alabama White Sauce?

Alabama White Sauce is a regional BBQ specialty native to Northern Alabama. It is a mayonnaise-based sauce mixed with vinegar, black pepper, and other spices. It is tangy, creamy, and is traditionally served over smoked chicken or turkey.

Is Moe's Original BBQ related to Moe's Southwest Grill?

No, they are completely separate companies. Moe's Original BBQ was founded by three friends from the University of Alabama and serves Southern BBQ. Moe's Southwest Grill serves customizable Tex-Mex burritos and bowls.

Does Moe's Original BBQ serve alcohol?

Yes! Almost all Moe's Original BBQ locations feature a full bar. They are known for serving the "Bushwacker" (a frozen, creamy, chocolatey rum cocktail) as well as a great selection of local craft beers and Southern iced tea.

Are there gluten-free options on the menu?

Yes. All of their smoked meats (pork, chicken, turkey, ribs) are naturally gluten-free. Most of the sides, including the baked beans, potato salad, and marinated slaw, are also gluten-free. Just skip the buns, cornbread, mac & cheese, and banana pudding. (For Tex-Mex options, see our Moe's Southwest Grill Gluten-Free Guide).

Can I buy their BBQ sauces in bulk?

Yes, most locations sell their signature Red BBQ Sauce and Alabama White Sauce by the bottle or pint so you can take the flavors home.
