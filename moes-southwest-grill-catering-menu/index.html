📞 How to Order Moe's Catering

Ordering catering from Moe's Southwest Grill is easy. Follow these steps to plan your perfect event.

1️⃣ Select Your Location

Find your nearest Moe's Southwest Grill location using our locations directory. Check if your local Moe's offers catering services.

2️⃣ Plan Your Menu

Choose from party platters, burrito boxes, taco trays, and sides. Determine how many people you're serving and select items that fit your budget.

3️⃣ Call Your Local Moe's

Contact your local Moe's restaurant directly to discuss catering options, pricing, and special requests. Most locations require 24-48 hours advance notice.

4️⃣ Customize Your Order

Tell the catering specialist about dietary restrictions, protein preferences, and any special customizations needed for your event.

5️⃣ Confirm Delivery or Pickup

Arrange delivery or pickup time that works best for your event. Ask about delivery fees, setup, and what's included in your catering package.

6️⃣ Enjoy Your Event!

Sit back and enjoy fresh, delicious Moe's catering while your guests enjoy authentic Mexican cuisine.

❓ Catering FAQ

What is the minimum order for Moe's catering?

Most Moe's locations require a minimum catering order of $150-$200. However, minimums may vary by location. Contact your local Moe's restaurant directly for specific catering minimums and ordering requirements.

How far in advance should I order catering?

For best availability, place your Moe's catering order at least 24-48 hours in advance. For larger orders (50+ people) or special events, ordering 5-7 days ahead is recommended. Contact your local restaurant for rush order availability.

Does Moe's deliver catering?

Yes, many Moe's locations offer catering delivery for orders. Delivery fees and radius vary by location. Confirm delivery options and fees when placing your order with your local Moe's restaurant.

Can I customize catering items?

Absolutely! Moe's is known for customization. You can modify ingredients, proteins, and toppings on catering items. Discuss customizations when ordering - some adjustments may affect pricing.

Do you offer vegetarian catering options?

Yes! Moe's offers vegetarian options including veggie burritos, bean platters, vegetable quesadillas, and vegetable bowls. All items can be customized to accommodate dietary preferences and restrictions.

What's included in catering delivery?

Most Moe's catering includes the food items, serving utensils, napkins, and condiments. Confirm what's included in your specific order. Setup and cleanup are typically the responsibility of the customer.

Can I mix proteins in bulk orders?

Yes! For larger catering orders like burrito boxes or bowl platters, you can typically mix different proteins. For example, in a 20-pack burrito box you could order 10 chicken and 10 steak. Confirm options when placing your order.

Do you cater to dietary restrictions?

Moe's can accommodate many dietary restrictions including vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free concerns, and nut allergies. Discuss specific needs when ordering so your catering is prepared safely.

What's the best catering for large corporate events?

For corporate events, DIY stations, burrito boxes, and large bowl platters work great since guests can customize their meals. These options are also efficient for quick serving. Build-your-own stations encourage team interaction.

How much should I order per person?

A good rule of thumb: order 1 burrito, taco (1.5-2 tacos), or bowl per person. If serving appetizers (nachos, guac) beforehand, you might order slightly less. For mixed events, order 25% more than your headcount to ensure plenty of food.
