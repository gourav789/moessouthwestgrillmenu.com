Hosting a tailgate, wedding, or office lunch in Trussville? The BBQ location is heavily equipped for catering. They offer:

❓ 7. Extensive Trussville FAQs

Because having two restaurants with the same name in one town causes endless confusion, we have compiled the most frequently asked questions from Trussville locals and visitors.

1. Are Moe's Original BBQ and Moe's Southwest Grill owned by the same company?

No. They are completely unrelated companies. Moe's Original BBQ was founded by three friends from the University of Alabama and focuses on Southern soul food and smoked meats. Moe's Southwest Grill is a national corporate chain focused on customizable Tex-Mex burritos.

2. Which Moe's in Trussville has the famous white cheese dip?

That is Moe's Southwest Grill. They are famous for their piping hot "Queso," which is a staple side dish for dipping your tortilla chips.

3. Which Moe's has the "Alabama White Sauce"?

That is Moe's Original BBQ. Alabama White Sauce is a regional mayonnaise and vinegar-based BBQ sauce that is traditionally served over smoked chicken and turkey.

4. Does Moe's Southwest Grill offer free chips?

Yes! One of the biggest perks of dining at the Southwest Grill location is that every single entrée order comes with a free basket of corn tortilla chips and unlimited access to the fresh salsa bar.

5. Does the BBQ location serve alcohol?

Yes, the Moe's Original BBQ location on Main Street in Trussville typically features a full bar, serving local craft beers, Southern iced tea, and their famous frozen "Bushwacker" cocktails.

6. I have Celiac disease. Which restaurant is safer?

Both require caution due to cross-contamination, but Moe's Southwest Grill is generally very easy to navigate for Celiacs if you follow the proper ordering protocols (requesting a glove change and fresh lettuce from the back). Read our full Gluten-Free Guide for detailed instructions. At the BBQ joint, almost all the smoked meats are naturally GF, but you must avoid the buns, cornbread, and mac & cheese.

7. Can I use a Moe's Southwest Grill gift card at Moe's Original BBQ?

No. Because they are entirely separate, unaffiliated businesses, gift cards and loyalty app rewards cannot be transferred between the two restaurants.
