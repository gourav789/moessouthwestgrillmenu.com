📖 1. Local History & White Oak Location

Located right on Highway 80 in East Texas, Moe's Pizza has been a cornerstone of the White Oak dining community for decades. Established in 1998, this independent, family-operated establishment has built a fierce local following that spans generations of White Oak residents.

Historically managed by the Hasan family, the restaurant prides itself on its hands-on approach to cooking. Unlike massive corporate chains that rely on frozen ingredients, the team at Moe's Pizza White Oak still makes their dough from scratch every single day, hand-cuts their fresh vegetables, and crafts their sauces in-house. It is a quintessential American small-town pizzeria that punches far above its weight in culinary quality.

Location Details:
📍 109 W US Highway 80, White Oak, TX 75693
📞 Phone: (903) 297-3000
Situated near the main intersection of White Oak, locals often describe it as being "right down the road from the Whataburger."

🥙 4. Authentic Gyros & Hot Subs

One of the most surprising and beloved aspects of this East Texas pizzeria is their incredible Greek-inspired menu. The gyros here are considered some of the best in the area.

  • Classic Greek Gyro (~$8.99): Slices of seasoned beef and lamb roasted on a spit, served on warm, fluffy pita bread with fresh lettuce, tomatoes, onions, and a generous pour of house-made tzatziki (cucumber-yogurt) sauce.
  • Chicken Gyro (~$8.99): Tender grilled chicken served on pita with the same fresh vegetables and tzatziki sauce.
  • Meatball Sub (~$9.50): Hearty Italian meatballs smothered in marinara sauce and melted mozzarella cheese, toasted on a fresh hoagie roll.
  • Philly Cheesesteak Sub (~$9.99): Thinly sliced steak grilled with onions and green peppers, topped with melted provolone cheese.
  • Italian Sub (~$9.50): Pepperoni, salami, ham, and provolone cheese, toasted and topped with lettuce, tomato, and Italian dressing.

❓ 7. Extensive Frequently Asked Questions

We have gathered the most common questions regarding the Moe's Pizza White Oak location to help you plan your next visit or delivery order.

1. Is Moe's Pizza in White Oak affiliated with the Moe's Southwest Grill chain?

No, they are completely independent. Moe's Pizza is a locally owned, family-operated Italian and Greek restaurant in Texas. Moe's Southwest Grill is a national corporate Tex-Mex chain.

2. Does Moe's Pizza offer delivery in White Oak?

Yes! Moe's Pizza offers local delivery to the White Oak area. They also offer fast takeout and have a comfortable dining room if you prefer to eat in.

3. Do they make their own pizza dough?

Yes, one of the reasons the community loves Moe's is because they make their dough fresh in-house every day, ensuring a superior crust compared to frozen alternatives.

4. What is the most popular item on the menu?

While the Supreme Pizza is a massive seller, the authentic Greek Gyro is arguably the hidden gem of the menu. Locals highly recommend it!

5. Do they serve alcohol?

Moe's Pizza is a family-oriented, casual dining establishment. They do not typically serve alcohol, focusing instead on a large selection of fountain sodas and iced tea.

6. Are there vegetarian options available?

Yes. You can order the Veggie Delight Pizza, the Cheese Ravioli, a large Greek Salad, or customize your own pizza with their extensive list of fresh vegetable toppings.

7. What are their hours of operation?

While hours can fluctuate, they are generally open for lunch and dinner seven days a week. It is always best to call (903) 297-3000 to verify their exact hours for holidays or special events.

8. Can I get catering for a local event?

Yes, local independent pizzerias like Moe's are fantastic for catering local school events, church gatherings, and office parties. Call the restaurant ahead of time to discuss large bulk orders of pizzas and pasta trays.
