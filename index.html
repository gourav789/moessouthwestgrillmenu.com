🏛️ Restaurant Overview & History

Moe's Southwest Grill is one of America's most beloved fast-casual restaurant chains, renowned for its bold Southwestern flavors, freshly prepared ingredients, and its iconic energetic greeting — "Welcome to Moe's!" — shouted by every team member whenever a guest walks through the door. Founded on December 9, 2000 in Atlanta, Georgia, Moe's has grown to over 570 locations across the United States, serving millions of loyal fans every year.

📜 Our Story

Moe's was founded by Martin Sprock and Raving Brands in 2000. The name "Moe's" is an acronym for Musicians, Outlaws, and Entertainers, reflecting the chain's original rock music-themed atmosphere. Today it is owned by GoTo Foods (formerly Focus Brands), which also manages Cinnabon, Auntie Anne's, and Jamba.

🌯 Our Menu Philosophy

Moe's is built around customization and freshness. Every item — from burritos and bowls to tacos and quesadillas — is built to order in front of you. The assembly-line format lets guests craft their perfect meal every single visit.

🎯 The Moe's Difference

Unlike most fast-casual competitors, Moe's famously provides free chips and salsa with every entrée order. This long-standing tradition has become a signature brand promise and a major reason guests return again and again. Their house-made queso is also legendary among regulars.

🏢 Scale & Footprint

With over 570+ franchise and corporate locations primarily across the southeastern United States — highest density in Georgia and Florida — Moe's has established itself as a powerhouse in the fast-casual Mexican food segment. International expansion into markets like India is now underway.

🏆 Brand Strengths

Moe's consistently scores high for ingredient freshness, value, and customization. The Moe Rewards loyalty program rewards repeat visitors, and the brand frequently runs limited-time deals, BOGO events, and seasonal menu items to keep the experience exciting.

🤝 Parent Company

Moe's is owned by GoTo Foods, a multi-brand portfolio operator. GoTo Foods' stable of well-known brands provides Moe's with robust operational support, marketing resources, and supply-chain efficiencies that keep quality consistent across all franchise locations.

🌶️ The Queso Experience

Moe's queso is a warm, smooth, mild-to-medium spiced white cheese dip served with freshly made tortilla chips. It's the kind of queso that has people coming back to Moe's just for this one item, ordering it as an appetizer, a side, or even as a protein topper on their burrito.

🔍 Moe's vs. The Competition

How does Moe's Southwest Grill stack up against other leading fast-casual Mexican and Southwest-style chains? Here's an honest comparison.

🌯 Moe's vs. Chipotle

Value: Moe's wins with FREE chips & salsa every visit, plus the Moe Value Meal option.

Customization: Moe's offers more topping variety and a wider selection of proteins.

Queso: Moe's famous queso vs. Chipotle's queso — Moe's fans say it's not even close.

Price: Comparable, though Moe's free chips perk gives it a value edge.

🌮 Moe's vs. Qdoba

Queso: Both chains are famous for queso — a very tight comparison that comes down to personal taste.

Atmosphere: Moe's vibrant "Welcome to Moe's!" culture feels more energetic and welcoming.

Menu Variety: Qdoba includes guacamole at no extra charge; Moe's charges for guac but provides free chips.

Loyalty Program: Both have solid rewards programs for frequent diners.

🥙 Moe's vs. Taco Bell

Price: Taco Bell wins on rock-bottom pricing, but Moe's easily wins on ingredient quality and freshness.

Quality: Moe's uses significantly fresher ingredients prepared in-store daily.

Customization: Moe's assembly-line model gives far more topping and protein choices.

Dining Experience: Moe's fast-casual experience is several tiers above Taco Bell's QSR format.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions About Moe's Southwest Grill

Does Moe's really give free chips and salsa with every order? ▼

Yes! Moe's famously provides free chips and salsa with every entrée purchase. This is one of Moe's most beloved brand promises and a major differentiator from competitors. It has been a Moe's tradition since the early days of the chain and remains a core part of the dining experience today.

What is Moe's most popular menu item? ▼

The Homewrecker Burrito is consistently Moe's best-selling and most popular menu item nationwide. It's a fully loaded burrito featuring your choice of protein, seasoned rice, black beans, shredded cheese, lettuce, pico de gallo, sour cream, and guacamole. Moe's Famous Queso is a close second in terms of fan obsession.

What does "Moe's" stand for? ▼

Moe's is an acronym for Musicians, Outlaws, and Entertainers. The chain was founded in 2000 in Atlanta, Georgia, by Martin Sprock with an original music-themed concept inspired by rock culture. While the brand has evolved over the years, the name has stuck and become one of the most recognisable in fast-casual dining.

How much does a typical Moe's meal cost? ▼

A typical Moe's entrée costs between $11.00 and $14.00, depending on your location and chosen protein. Signature items like the Homewrecker range from $13.00–$16.50. Adding guacamole costs $2.00–$3.50 extra, and a cup of the famous queso runs $3.00–$5.00. Remember that chips and salsa are always FREE with any entrée purchase.

Does Moe's have vegetarian and vegan options? ▼

Yes! Moe's has excellent plant-based options. The Edgy Veggie Burrito (with seasoned tofu) is Moe's most popular vegetarian item. You can also choose tofu or a no-protein option (beans + rice + veggies only) for any build-your-own item. Many toppings like guacamole, pico de gallo, beans, rice, and salsa are naturally vegan. Always confirm specific allergen or vegan preparation protocols with your local Moe's.

Can I order Moe's online or through delivery apps? ▼

Absolutely! You can order online directly through moes.com or the Moe's mobile app for both pickup and delivery. Moe's also partners with major third-party delivery services including DoorDash, Uber Eats, and Grubhub. Keep in mind that prices on delivery platforms may be slightly higher than in-store pricing due to service fees and platform markups.

What is the Moe Rewards program? ▼

The Moe Rewards program is Moe's loyalty program that lets you earn points on every purchase. New members typically receive a free cup of queso as a welcome bonus upon sign-up. Points accumulate with each visit and can be redeemed for free food, discounts, and exclusive deals. Sign up through the Moe's website or mobile app to start earning immediately.

How many Moe's Southwest Grill locations are there? ▼

Moe's Southwest Grill has over 570 locations across the United States, with the highest concentration in the southeastern states — particularly Georgia and Florida. The brand is expanding internationally as well, with new markets like India being entered in recent years. Use the store locator at moes.com to find your nearest location.

Does Moe's offer catering? ▼

Yes! Moe's offers catering services for corporate events, office lunches, parties, graduations, sports events, and more. Popular catering options include burrito bars, taco stations, and bowl set-ups where guests can customise their own meals. Contact your local Moe's or visit the official website to place a catering order or get a quote.
