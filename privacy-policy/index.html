What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help us remember your preferences and track your browsing behavior.

Types of Cookies We Use

  • Analytical Cookies: Help us understand how visitors use our website (e.g., Google Analytics).
  • Advertising Cookies: Allow third-party advertisers to track your behavior and show you targeted ads.
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your website preferences and settings.

Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through browser settings. You can:

  • Accept or reject cookies
  • Delete cookies from your device
  • Set your browser to notify you when a cookie is being set
  • Use private/incognito browsing mode to avoid storing cookies

Disabling cookies may limit your ability to use certain features of our website. Some essential functionality may not work properly without cookies.
