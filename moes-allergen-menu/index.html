🛡️ Moe's Southwest Grill Allergen Menu Guide 2026

Navigating dining out with food allergies or dietary restrictions can be stressful, but the highly customizable Moe's Southwest Grill menu makes it significantly easier. Whether you're avoiding gluten, dairy, nuts, or animal products, Moe's offers a wide array of fresh ingredients that can be tailored to fit your specific needs.

💡 Why Moe's is Allergy-Friendly: Because every meal is built-to-order right in front of you, you have complete control over what goes into your bowl, burrito, or taco. Plus, most Moe's locations do not use peanuts or tree nuts in their standard menu items, making it a popular choice for families managing nut allergies.

⚠️ Important Cross-Contact Notice

While Moe's provides detailed allergen information, it is critical to understand the environment of a fast-casual assembly line.

Moe's explicitly warns that because allergens are present in their kitchens and shared prep areas, there is a potential risk of cross-contamination. Ingredients like cheese, flour tortillas, and sour cream are kept in close proximity to safe ingredients like lettuce, salsas, and beans. Bits of cheese can easily fall into the lettuce bin during a busy lunch rush.

🚨 The Golden Rule: If you have a severe food allergy, you must notify the restaurant staff directly before placing your order. Ask the manager to change their gloves and pull fresh ingredients from the back (undisturbed pans) to minimize cross-contact risks.

🌾 Gluten-Friendly Options

Moe's is a fantastic option for those avoiding gluten (for a deeper dive, read our Ultimate Celiac & GF Guide), provided you skip the flour tortillas and take precautions against cross-contact.

Note on Chips: The tortilla chips are made from corn and are naturally gluten-free, but they are fried in the same oil that may be used for other items in some locations. Check with your local store if you are highly sensitive (Celiac).

🧀 Dairy-Free Options

Avoiding dairy at Moe's is straightforward: simply skip the cheese, sour cream, and queso. The remaining ingredients are rich and flavorful enough that you won't even miss the dairy.

  • ✅ Safe Proteins: Chicken, Steak, Ground Beef, Tofu
  • ✅ Safe Bases: Flour Tortillas, Corn Shells, Rice, Beans, Chips
  • ✅ Safe Flavor Additions: Guacamole, Pico de Gallo, Hard Rock 'N' Roll Sauce, and all fresh salsas from the salsa bar.
  • ❌ AVOID: Shredded Cheese, Sour Cream, Moe's Famous Queso, Chipotle Ranch.

💡 Pro Tip: To make up for the creaminess of sour cream and cheese, ask for an extra scoop of fresh guacamole and use the mild salsa verde!

🌱 Vegan and Vegetarian Menu

Moe's is one of the most vegan-friendly fast-casual chains. Since you customize your own meal, building a filling, plant-based dish is incredibly easy.

Building a Vegan Meal

Start with a Burrito, Bowl, Salad, or Tacos.

  • Protein: Choose the Organic Tofu (seasoned with Southwestern spices) or go double beans.
  • Rice & Beans: Both the Black Beans and Pinto Beans are vegan (no lard). The Cilantro-Lime Rice is also vegan.
  • Toppings: Load up on shredded lettuce, grilled onions and peppers, black olives, jalapeños, fresh cilantro, and cucumbers.
  • Finishers: Guacamole is 100% vegan, as are all the standard salsas at the salsa bar.

*Vegetarians can enjoy everything vegans do, plus the addition of Queso, Shredded Cheese, and Sour Cream. The Art Vandalay burrito is a popular standard vegetarian choice.

🥜 Top 9 Food Allergens Breakdown

Here is a quick reference for the most common food allergies based on Moe's standard ingredient lists.

🥜 Peanuts & Tree Nuts
Moe's standard menu items do not contain peanuts or tree nuts. However, some limited-time desserts (like certain cookies) may pose a risk. Verify with the manager.
🥚 Eggs
Eggs are typically only found in certain dressings (like Chipotle Ranch) or baked desserts (cookies/brownies). The main proteins, rice, beans, and tortillas are egg-free.
🐟 Fish & Shellfish
Moe's does not typically serve fish or shellfish as part of their standard menu, making it a very safe environment for seafood allergies.
🌱 Soy
Soy is present in the Tofu, obviously. It may also be present in the cooking oil or certain marinades/sauces. If you have a severe soy allergy, ask for the ingredient binder.

💡 How to Safely Order at Moe's

4 Steps for Allergy Safety

1. Speak Up Immediately: Tell the greeter (the person who says "Welcome to Moe's!") about your allergy before they start making your food.

2. Ask for a Glove Change: Politely request that the employee making your food washes their hands and puts on a fresh pair of gloves.

3. Request Fresh Ingredients: Ask if they can pull ingredients from backup pans in the back or underneath the counter to avoid the cross-contaminated line bins.

4. Skip the Salsa Bar: The self-serve salsa bar is highly prone to cross-contact from other guests. Ask the staff for a side of fresh salsa directly from the kitchen.

❓ Allergy FAQs

Are Moe's chips gluten-free?

Yes, the tortilla chips are made entirely from corn. However, they are fried in-house. If you have Celiac disease, ask if they fry any gluten-containing items in the same oil (like flour tortilla salad bowls or chimichangas).

Does Moe's use peanut oil?

No, Moe's typically uses soybean or a vegetable oil blend for cooking and frying. They are widely considered a peanut-free facility for their main menu items.

Is Moe's Queso pasteurized?

Yes. All cheeses served at Moe's, including the famous Queso, shredded cheese, and sour cream, are made with pasteurized milk, making them safe for pregnant guests.

Is the Tofu gluten-free?

Yes, the organic tofu served at Moe's is seasoned with gluten-free spices and does not contain wheat soy sauce.
