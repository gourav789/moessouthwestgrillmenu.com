📍 About Moe's Southwest Grill in Las Vegas

Moe's Southwest Grill is a fast-casual Tex-Mex chain founded in 2000 in Atlanta, Georgia. It now operates hundreds of locations across dozens of states, including Nevada. The concept is simple but effective: fresh ingredients, grilled-to-order proteins, and complete customization with more than 20 toppings, salsas, and sauces.

🔥 Signature Burritos at Moe's Las Vegas

Burritos are the heart of the Moe's menu — big, warm flour tortillas stuffed with everything you love. Here are the most iconic options:

The Homewrecker Burrito ⭐ Most Popular

The Homewrecker is the signature burrito that put Moe's on the map. It comes loaded with fresh guacamole (included!), your choice of protein (or even two), cilantro-lime rice, black or pinto beans, shredded cheese, shredded romaine, pico de gallo, and sour cream. It's hearty, flavorful, and the guacamole makes every bite bright and creamy.

Why it's iconic: Many regulars add a drizzle of Moe's Sauce or Chipotle Ranch for extra kick. The two-protein option creates incredible flavor complexity. First-timers always say "this is what I came for!"

Joey Bag of Donuts

A longtime favorite for those who want a straightforward, satisfying burrito without the guacamole surcharge. It typically features tender protein (classically chicken for many fans), rice, beans, shredded cheese, and pico de gallo. It's generous but not overly complicated — perfect when you want classic Tex-Mex done right.

Art Vandalay (Vegetarian)

The go-to vegetarian burrito, packed with beans, rice, cheese, and plenty of fresh vegetables and salsas. It proves you don't need meat to get a filling, flavorful meal. Customize with tofu for extra protein.

The Close Talker

Another named classic that fans remember fondly — often associated with bold, fresh-forward flavors. Sometimes enjoyed as a salad or burrito-style option heavy on the crisp toppings and zesty salsas. Request it customized to your preferences.

💡 Customization Tip: All burritos can be customized endlessly. Want it milder or extra spicy? Use the salsa bar to finish it yourself. Most toppings (pico, lettuce, cilantro, jalapeños) are completely free!

🌮 Tacos, Quesadillas & More

Beyond burritos and bowls, Moe's offers diverse options for every craving and occasion.

Tacos

Tacos are a lighter, handheld option that still lets you customize everything. Choose soft flour or crispy corn shells and fill them with adobo chicken, steak, ground beef, or tofu. The 3-Taco Combo is a smart value — three tacos plus rice and beans on the side.

  • 🍗 Chicken tacos: Shine with adobo seasoning and fresh pico
  • 🥩 Steak tacos: Get a nice char and pair beautifully with roasted corn salsa or guacamole
  • 🍖 Ground beef: Offers classic comfort and familiar flavors
  • 🌱 Tofu tacos: Surprise many people with how well the seasoning works

Quesadillas

Grilled flour tortillas stuffed with cheese and your choice of protein, then cut into wedges. They're crispy on the outside and gooey inside — great for sharing or as a satisfying solo meal. Load them with lettuce, cheese, sour cream, and your favorite salsas from the bar.

Nachos

Fresh tortilla chips topped with Moe's Famous Queso, protein, beans, lettuce, pico, jalapeños, and more. They're shareable and fun, especially when you add guacamole and extra sour cream. Perfect for sharing with friends or as a solo indulgence.

Vegetarian & Vegan Options

Moe's makes it easy to eat meat-free. Organic-style tofu is a solid protein choice that absorbs the seasonings well. Bean-based meals (black or pinto) with rice, all the fresh toppings, guacamole, and salsas are naturally vegetarian and filling.

For vegan orders, simply skip cheese, sour cream, and queso, then load up on guacamole, beans, rice, lettuce, pico, and the full salsa bar. Many locations are happy to accommodate and the staff is used to custom requests.

Gluten-Friendly Choices

Bowl and salad options are naturally gluten-friendly since they skip the flour tortilla. You can still enjoy rice, beans, proteins, cheese, guacamole, and salsas. Corn tortilla tacos may also work for some, but always confirm with the location about shared prep surfaces. For a complete guide on cross-contact and safe ordering, read our Moe's Gluten-Free Menu Guide.

Does Moe's Southwest Grill have locations in Las Vegas?
