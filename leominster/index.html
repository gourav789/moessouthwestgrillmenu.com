❓ FAQs About Moe's Leominster

What are the hours?

Monday–Sunday, 10:30 AM to 9:00 PM. The location is open 7 days a week for your convenience. Call (978) 401-2657 to confirm hours on holidays.

Does Moe's Leominster deliver?

Yes! Use DoorDash, Uber Eats, or Grubhub for delivery. You can also order online at moes.com for pickup or delivery.

What's the most popular item?

The Homewrecker Burrito is Moe's #1 best-seller. Moe's Famous Queso is a close second — people rave about it constantly.
